# Golden Set Annotation Guideline — Draft

This guideline becomes operational only after recording access, privacy approval and annotator assignment.

## Principles

- Never infer content that is not audible.
- Use `UNKNOWN` when audio, role or wording is uncertain.
- Preserve exact numbers, units, product names and timestamps.
- Do not place full phone numbers, customer names or raw audio in Git.
- Customer `NEW/RETURNING` status must come from authoritative context, not the annotator's impression.

## Required labels

- speaker turns and `AGENT/CUSTOMER/UNKNOWN` role;
- product, grade, dimension, quantity and price;
- delivery, freight, tax and payment terms;
- objection and competitor mention;
- next action and owner;
- needs-discovery and closing observations;
- known outcome when an authoritative invoice/quote label exists.

Two annotators should independently label the held-out evaluation subset. Disagreements are adjudicated and retained as audit metadata.
